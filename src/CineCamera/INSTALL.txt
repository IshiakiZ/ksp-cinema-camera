Cinema Camera @VERSION@
for Kerbal Space Program 1.12.x

The flight camera worked like a real one: lens, sensor, aperture, focus, shutter and film speed, and a viewfinder to look through.

INSTALL
  Copy both folders in this download (Keystone and CineCamera) into your KSP's
  GameData folder, and start the game. Keystone is the base this mod stands on; one copy
  serves every mod that uses it. To remove the mod, delete the CineCamera folder.

USE
  Open the Keystone window: the keystone button on the game's toolbar, or Option-K on a
  Mac and Alt-K elsewhere. Then turn to Cinema Camera and switch it on.

WHERE IT WORKS
  Made and tried on a Mac, and tried on Windows 11: Keystone's lens (its shader built for
  Direct3D), the viewfinder, the bars and the moves. Nobody has run it on Linux yet.

Everything else: https://github.com/IshiakiZ/ksp-cinema-camera
