Cinema Camera @VERSION@
for Kerbal Space Program 1.12.x

The flight camera worked like a real one: lens, sensor, aperture, focus, shutter and film speed, and a viewfinder to look through.
From 0.7.0: 39 real cameras with their own screens and logs, LUTs laid on live, lens
flares from lamps, and the moves launches are filmed with.

INSTALL
  This mod needs Keystone 0.11 or later, the base it stands on, which is a separate download:
    https://spacedock.info/mod/4665/Keystone
  Copy Keystone's folder (Keystone) into your KSP's GameData folder, and the CineCamera
  folder from this download beside it, and start the game. Without Keystone the game does
  not load this mod. One copy of Keystone serves every mod that uses it. To remove this
  mod, delete the CineCamera folder.

USE
  Open the Keystone window: the keystone button on the game's toolbar, or Option-K on a
  Mac and Alt-K elsewhere. Then turn to Cinema Camera and switch it on.

WHERE IT WORKS
  Made and tried on a Mac, and tried on Windows 11 (Direct3D 11 and OpenGL), with TUFX
  and without it: on 2026-10-20 a camera with its LUT, false colour and focus peaking on
  both. The lens flares and the new camera moves have been run on the Mac only. Nobody
  has run it on Linux yet.

Everything else: https://github.com/IshiakiZ/ksp-cinema-camera
